Story

Nightmare in Prison

This is an American town of 40,000 people up north.

One day in the early morning, a cardboard box was found in front of Mr. F's house.
In that box, there were pieces of human flesh...
A series of murders had started after this.

A cleaner found two bodies in a municipal pool two days later.
The stomachs of the bodies were ripped to shreds with something incisive like a bear claw.



The serial killings took place three days later and then again four days later. Two policemen on night duty were killed on the sixth day.
In response to this horrible situation, a curfew was imposed in the city, but the number of victims increased one by one every day. The whole city was plunged into a state of fear.

The legend of the werewolf had been told for a long time in this region, so people began to wonder if these horrible killings weren't the work of a werewolf.

After 3 weeks, the police tracked down a few human suspects and took them into an abandoned prison.
In this isolated prison, the suspects were given a mission that they find and execute the werewolves.


What was given to them was one pistol, supernatural power, and the judgment to see the truth.
Now the battle of humans and werewolves has started in this prison to survive the nightmare.

How to Play

About Werewolf Game
In this game, the players work together to find out which of them is the Werewolf. At the end, the players take a vote to decide who they all think the Werewolf is.
All the players are divided into two teams, Citizens and Werewolves. Each team tries to destroy the other.
How to Win
Citizens:
Citizens win when they execute all of the werewolves.

Werewolves:
Warewolves win when the number of werewolves and citizens becomes the same.

Vampires:
Vampires win when they survive until the game is over. Vampires do not count as either Citizen or Werewolf.
How to start the game
1. Choose a Game Master
First of all, choose a Game Master who rules the game.
The Game Master reads the rules of the game shown on the display screen for other players.

2. Player Setting
All the players sit and make a circle.
Firstly, the Game Master entries his or her profile. After the Game Master, all the players entry their profile.

3. Night stage
Each player confirms his or her own role after the device is handed to them. The player then hands the device to the next person. The role assigned to that person will determine what night action he or she will take.

4. Morning stage
The Game Master announces to everyone which player was attacked by the Werewolf at night.
The rest of the players talk to each other to find out who the Werewolf could be.

5. Judgment
Each player announces the name of a player who is thought to be a werewolf.
The player who is voted the most likely to be the Werewolf will be executed.

6. Game Over
By repeating the night, morning, and judgment loop as described above, you try to kill the players who are on the other team.
Then game is over when either the Citizens or Werewolves meet the criteria for winning.

Option Rule

No killing on the 1st night
We recommend the "No killing on the 1st night" mode is available. No one is killed on the 1st night, all players can enjoy the 1st morning meeting.
If this mode is on, players involved with the psycho killer on the 1st night do not die.
If this mode is on, Vampire Bat does not die when the fortune teller reads the "Vampire Bat" on the 1st night. Even if this mode is on, if there are two or more Werewolves and Magician replaces a Werewolf card, the Werewolf whose werewolf card is stolen dies.
Foretelling on the 1st night
"Foretelling on the 1st night" can be adjusted to balance the power with the Werewolf team.
If the number of participants is small, "No Foretelling" is recommended.
If the number of people is around 10, "Knowing a human" is recommended.
Reducing a role randomly
One of roles (except "Werewolf", "Vampire", "Vampire Bat", "Lovers", "Destined Lovers") maybe changes to Citizen. However, if a Citizen may be changed to a Citizen, nothing changes.
The voting style
You select the voting style.
- Seat order voting
　The players will vote in the seat order starting from the player who died last.

- Free voting
The order of voting is not decided. Whoever wants to vote should rasie his or her hand.

- Anonymous voting
The players hand over the device and vote in turn so as not to know who voted on whom.
Judgement when there is a tie
You can select the judgement rule after two consecutive ties.

- Kill at random
　Only one player is chosen to be killed at random.

- No killing
None of the players will be killed in this stage.

- Selectable
The players vote to decide the judgement rule, random killing or no killing. If the number of votes for "Random Killing" is ties to "No Kill", the judgement will be "Random Killing".
Skip checking answers
The deceased player will automatically skip checking the answer. Use this if you want the deceased player to continue guessing until the end of the game.
Player Points
Players earn player points based on the game results and can compete for points.

1. Winning points
When Citizens win, all of the players on the Citizens' team get 10 points.
When Werewolves win, all of the players on the Werewolves' team get 15 points.
(When Vampires win, all of the players on the Vampires' team get 20 points.)

2. Survival points
Every morning, the players who survived get 1 point.

3. Fast target bonus points
The first player to be killed by a werewolf gets a bonus 3 points for being a threat to the werewolf.
- You can change the setting of the each points.

More Details

Faul play
To enjoy this game, we explain the unfair actions.

1. Asking about the content of the night action screens and the visuals on the cards.
Night actions differ by player. So you can know the liar by asking about the visual of the game screen.

2. Announcing the result of the night actions the next morning.
In this game you can freely chat during the night. However, superpower players (ex: Fortune Teller, Shaman) can't tell the results of the night actions until the next morning.

3. Checking the player whom other players suspect.
In the morning, the most suspicious player is called. If you check the various suspects from different players, you can narrow down the liar to a few players.
About the night actions
Q. If Werewolves select different players, does this mean that several players are killed?
No. Only one citizen will be killed by voting. Of the top-voted citizens, one of them will be killed at random.

Q. When the Fortune Teller checks the player in special positions, can the Fortune Teller know the role of the player?
No. The Fortune Teller can know only if the player is Werewolf or human.

Q. When two Knights select the different players, are the several players protected?
No. Only one player will be protected at random.

Q. When two Werewolves select the psycho killer, are both of the Werewolves killed?
Yes. The werewolf who selects the psycho killer will be killed at that time.

Q. Why didn't the Fortune Teller die despite having checked the psycho killer?
When you set the option "No killing in the 1st night", the Fortune Teller doesn't die despite checking the psycho killer in the 1st night. The Werewolf and the Knight who come into contact with the psycho killer don't die either.

Q. Will the Magician die, when he comes into contact with the psycho killer?
No. The Magician can change his card for the psycho killer card. From the next morning the Magician player transforms to the psycho killer.

Q. When the Magician and the Werewolf attack each other, what will be the result of the night action?
When the Werewolf attacks the Magician, the Magician will die and doesn't change the cards.
The replacement of cards by the magician occurs only if the werewolf attacks someone other than the magician.

When over 2 werewolves are alive, the werewolf (or Mentalist) changed by magician will be killed by the other werewolf and the magician will transform to a werewolf (or Mentalist) from the next morning.
So in any case, only one player will die.

Q. Can the Great Shaman transform to "Vampire"?
No. the great shaman select the "Necromancy" button for "Vampire", you will transform to "Citizen". If the Vampire=>Human" Mode mode is turned on, the "Necromancy" button will appear on the vampire as well.
Online Mode
Q. Must all players install this app?
Yes. Players must install "Werewolf Nightmare in Prison" App.


Q. Must all players buy the paid cards which are used in the game?
No. The paid cards which the Game Master bought are available for all players.

Q. Can we play remotely with video chat?
Yes, you can enjoy werewolf game remotely using a variety of online chat rooms such as ZOOM or Discord. For specific instructions on how to play, please refer to this manual.

Q. How can I use the "Game Master Tool"?
The Game Master can kill the player who want sto retire from the game with the Game Master Tool.
Also the Game Master can make the various roles with the "Original" role. Please check the examples of the Original role on this page.
Changing The Role Name
Q. Can I change the role name?
Yes. Tap the role name in the card settings page, you can change the role name. However, the Werewolf and Citizen can not change the role name.
Example of Original Role
Preset: Change the original role's name.
You have to change the name of the "Original" role in the card settings page by tapping the name.

Ex: "Detective"
The "Detective" wins by guessing right about all the special skill role's players (Werewolf, Fortune Teller, etc).
After the second day, the "Detective" can confess that he or she is the "Detective." This stops the discussion in the day stage.
Then "Detective" says "I got it!" and explains all the special skill roles.
When the reasoning is prefectly correct, only the "Detective" becomes the winner.
But when the reasoning has something wrong, the "Detective" will die.
Please kill the "Detective" with Game Master Tool.
Caution :
The Game Master needs to know the answer of special roles to judge the "Detective's reasoning.
The first dead player can be Game Master and memorize the answer by checking the roles in the night stage.

Ex: "Heroine"
The "Heroine" is a citizen who has supernatural power.
After the second day, the "Heroine" can confess that the "Heroine" is herself and stop the discussion in the day stage.
Then "Heroine" can revive the dead player in exchange for her life.
Please kill the "Heroine" and revive the target dead player with the Game Master Tool.

Caution :
When using "Heroine", please set the "Skip Checking Answers" function on. This is so that the dead players don't find out the answers.

プレイヤーランキングとは
ゲーム内容によって得られたポイントの合計によるランキングです。ゲームを続けることで、各プレイヤーに加算されていきます。ぜひ、トッププレイヤーを目指しましょう。

ポイントの種類
勝利ポイント：
ゲームに勝利したチームの全員が得られるポイント。市民チームで勝利した場合、10ポイント。人狼チームで勝利した場合、15ポイントが加算されます。 吸血鬼チームで勝利した場合、20ポイントが加算されます。

生存ポイント：
生きて朝を迎えるたびに、1ポイント加算されます。

ファーストターゲットボーナス：
強いプレイヤーほど、人狼に狙われやすいため、最初に人狼に殺されたプレイヤーには、3ポイントが加算されます。

What is Player Ranking
It is a ranking of total points which you get in the game. Each player gets points as they play the game. Get more points and be a top player!

How to get points

Winning points:
The points which everyone in the winning team can get.
When you win the game as a citizen, you get 10 points.
When you win the game as a werewolf, you get 15 points. When you win the game as a vampire, you get 20 points.

Surivival points:
When you survive each night, you get 1 point.

First target bonus points:
Stronger players are more likely to be targeted by the werewolves.
If you are killed first, you get 3 points.
